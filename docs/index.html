How this is computed. The 2019 and 2024 distributions are rebuilt from the five published percentiles with monotone interpolation. Participation and the treated effect vary linearly with prior rank; the average across all students stays at the values set above, and participation is capped at 100 percent with the excess seats handed to percentiles below the cap.

Group follows the students who started at percentile p. A share of them is treated, so the group gains that share times the effect. Distribution asks where whoever stands at percentile p after the program is. Treated students pass untreated neighbors, so a partial program spreads the distribution and the two readings differ. The two agree when everyone or no one at a percentile is treated.

Assumptions: within a percentile, who takes part is unrelated to how much they would gain; the chart covers p10 to p90, the range the published percentiles support. Share undone above 100 percent means the percentile ends above its 2019 level. Scores are NAEP scale points, and requirements and effects are 2019 SD units. The R pipeline in analysis/ is the reference implementation; analysis/tests/test-tool-engine.mjs checks this page's engine against it.
