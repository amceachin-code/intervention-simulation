Math, grade 8 · 2019 to 2024

Same seats, same boost. Where the seats go decides whether the gap closes.

Each rule below gives a seat to 28% of students, and each student with a seat gains 0.155 SD. Results are counted across all students, the tool's main measure.

How to count

Lowest scorers first

Seats go to the lowest-scoring students until they run out. A limit, not a plan: no real screen picks students this precisely.

Two rules leave the gap wider than no program at all (orange). With an equal chance, students who take part move past classmates who do not, which spreads scores out. With sign-up, higher scorers take more of the seats.
